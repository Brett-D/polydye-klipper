Controls

The dry run homes if needed, lifts the nozzle to 20 mm and prints two test passes without firing any ink. Wipe moves the cartridge to the sponge at the right end of the X gantry, wipes it and comes back. Both move the printer; keep the bed clear. To stop the printer at once, press Emergency stop at the top of the page; Off only turns PolyDye off once the queued moves are done.

Settings polydye.cfg

Offsets are the cartridge nozzle-row centre minus the hotend nozzle, in bed coordinates. Direction trim moves the ink of reverse passes along the pass axis. The cartridge template is the nozzle waveform: a and b are two variants for each cartridge type, so try both at the nozzle check. A black template needs pass data made for a black cartridge. The fire pulse sets how long each nozzle is heated: longer pulses put more energy into each drop, for when the board's boost voltage is below the cartridge's. Leave it empty for the board's 6 (1.8 µs). Saving edits only the [polydye] section of polydye.cfg; Klipper applies it after a restart.

Ink position live nudges

Moves where the ink lands, from the next pass on, without a restart; it also works during a print. Compare the ink with the plastic it should sit on: if the ink is 0.1 mm too far left, press +X with 0.1 mm. Reverse passes moves only the passes printed in the reverse direction, to line them up with the forward ones. Nudges are lost at the next Klipper restart unless you press Store.

Board firmware

Updates go over the board's USB cable. Klipper loses the board for about half a minute while it restarts, then reconnects by itself. If the new firmware doesn't start properly, the board goes back to the previous one on its own. Not possible during a print.

Calibration

Fire test live ink on paper

Prints a stripe of every nozzle for each template and fire pulse, side by side, so you can see which settings put ink down. Tape paper on the bed and fit the cartridge first. The test homes if needed, lowers the nozzle to Z and puts the template and pulse back afterwards. Stripes run along the pass axis.

Spit test live ink, no motion

Fires every nozzle 1000 times a second for a few seconds without moving. Hold a folded paper towel under the cartridge, against the nozzles: a working cartridge leaves a coloured patch. It is also a steady load for a USB-C power meter on the board's power input (J4): compare the current with the cartridge in and out to see whether the nozzle heaters draw anything.

The spit test and the signal check need newer board firmware: update it under Board firmware.

Next steps

  1. Nozzle check: print every nozzle once to find blocked ones, with both templates (a and b).
  2. Cartridge offset: print ink marks next to a plastic reference and nudge X and Y until they line up.
  3. Direction alignment: print in both directions and nudge Reverse passes until the passes line up.

Signal check cartridge out, multimeter

Finds where each cartridge signal arrives, to check the chain from the daughter board through the FFC and the interface board to the cartridge pads. The page holds one line HIGH at a time; it should read its rail voltage at exactly one pogo pin and about 0 V everywhere else.

  1. Keep the cartridge out for the whole check.
  2. Power off. Slide the interface board out of the holder with its FFC still connected, and lay it face down, so the pogo pins' solder joints on its back face up. Power on again.
  3. Meter on DC volts, black lead on pin 8 (GND) of either CD4504 on the daughter board.
  4. Start turns the boost on in dry run (nothing fires). Then press High on a signal, find the joint that reads the expected voltage, and note it: the pogo's label, or its cluster (9 or 7 pins) and position counted from the FPC end. A line goes back to LOW after 5 minutes by itself.
  5. Finish puts every line LOW and the boost off. Then check the fire lines and ground below, with the power off, and save the results.

Fire lines and ground power off, continuity

PWRA and PWRB carry the nozzle heater pulses and are never held on. Turn the power off and set the meter to continuity. Beep from the drain of the AO3401 (marked “3401”) in each pulse limiter on the daughter board to the joints: on a SOT-23 the drain is pin 3, the single pin on its own side. PWRA comes from the F3 limiter, PWRB from the F5 limiter. Then beep CD4504 pin 8 to the joints: two of them are GND.
